Our Story

About Nachi Eng Ltd

Built by an engineer, for engineers — and the operations teams who depend on them.

From the factory floor
to the boardroom dashboard.

I'm Godswill — a multi-skilled maintenance engineer and the founder of Nachi Eng Ltd. After graduating with a First Class BEng (Hons) in Electrical & Electronic Engineering and working across factories, warehouses, hospitals and commercial sites, I kept seeing the same problem everywhere.

Maintenance teams drowning in paperwork. Assets breaking down unexpectedly. Engineers spending more time chasing information than actually doing the work. No visibility. No system. Just stress.

So I built the solution. At Nachi Eng Ltd, we combine real on-the-ground engineering expertise with a custom-built Maintenance Management System — giving operations teams full visibility, control and confidence over their maintenance function.

"If your maintenance operation needs to move from reactive to proactive — let's talk."

— Godswill C. Iweajunwa, Founder & Director
